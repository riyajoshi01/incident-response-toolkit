# Compromised Account Response Playbook

1. Validate suspicious login activity.
2. Disable or restrict the account if compromise is confirmed.
3. Revoke active sessions.
4. Reset the password.
5. Review MFA settings.
6. Check permissions and privilege changes.
7. Review mailbox rules and forwarding settings.
8. Search for additional compromised accounts.
9. Secure the account before restoring access.
10. Document the incident.
