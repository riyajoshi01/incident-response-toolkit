# Data Breach Response Playbook

1. Preserve relevant evidence.
2. Identify affected systems and information.
3. Determine which users or customers may be affected.
4. Investigate whether information was accessed or exfiltrated.
5. Involve legal and privacy personnel.
6. Review applicable notification requirements.
7. Coordinate approved notifications.
8. Continue monitoring for unauthorized activity.
9. Document findings and corrective actions.
