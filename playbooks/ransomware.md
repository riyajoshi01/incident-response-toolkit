# Ransomware Response Playbook

1. Isolate affected systems.
2. Identify potentially affected endpoints and servers.
3. Protect clean backups.
4. Preserve relevant evidence.
5. Identify the initial access method.
6. Check for compromised accounts.
7. Remove attacker access and persistence.
8. Address the exploited weakness.
9. Restore systems from trusted backups.
10. Monitor recovered systems.
11. Complete a post-incident review.
